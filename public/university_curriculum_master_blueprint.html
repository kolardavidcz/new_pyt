Showcase 1: The 13-Week Paced University Master (RECOMMENDED)

13 Active Teaching Weeks (W0..W12) + Curated W99 Shelf — Zero Overloaded Weeks

Engineered specifically to solve cognitive overload. Every single week is strictly budgeted to 25–35 live slides (~65–80 presentation minutes + 10 min lab intro). Non-essential setup tutorials, obsolete formats, and niche tooling are cleanly moved to the W99 Self-Study Shelf.

Target Pacing
13 Weeks × ~32 live slides ≈ 72 min/week
VŠCHT Praha / CTU University Computer Science & Engineering students (Prior C/C++/Java).
